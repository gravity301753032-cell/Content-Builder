## Key Quotes on AI Job Displacement (5-year horizon)

> "Timeline, I think it's within the next five years. But I look at what we're doing with some of our agentic technology. Say, a financial audit agent — it's going to replace the work of junior auditors. I look at what we're doing with our contract intelligence agent. It does replace the work of lawyers, or at least a subset of lawyers. I think we just have to be honest about it."

> "Am I worried about the job displacement? Absolutely. So we have to figure out what we're going to retrain these folks who get displaced into, or we're going to have a set of social issues."

> "I think you can automate a lot of the really heavy lifting on a financial audit... You could be closing the books continuously, and by the time you're closing the books, it's all done. That's just not something we could have done in the past without agentic technology. So what I'm seeing is almost a renaissance in the finance world because of AI."

> "Just like it happened in the Industrial Revolution. This one's just moving at a much faster clip."
