## 2. Founder Story (Why this matters for credibility)

Both founders came from **PeopleSoft**:
- **Dave Duffield** = PeopleSoft founder
- **Aneel Bhusri** = PeopleSoft Vice Chairman

When Oracle hostile-takeover'd PeopleSoft in 2005, the two co-founders left and started Workday. So Workday was literally built as "what we wish PeopleSoft had been — cloud-native, modern UI, AI-first".

This explains why Bhusri can speak credibly about AI + accounting: he has spent 20+ years building the system of record that AI companies are now adopting.

## 6. NetSuite vs Workday (CPA-relevant comparison)

| Dimension | NetSuite (Oracle) | Workday |
|-----------|-------------------|---------|
| **Parent** | Oracle (since 2016) | Independent (NASDAQ: WDAY) |
| **Origin** | Finance-first (1998) | HCM-first, extended to finance (2005) |
| **Target market** | Mid-market (SME → 5,000 employees) | Enterprise (1,000+ employees, F500 focus) |
| **Architecture** | Cloud, but older codebase | Cloud-native, modern stack |
| **HCM strength** | Moderate (added later) | **Strongest in market** |
| **Financial management** | **Deepest** (multi-entity, multibook, global) | Mature but historically HCM-first |
| **AI strategy** | Oracle's broader AI (less focused) | **AI-native**, 20 agents, Illuminate platform |
| **Industry fit** | Software, distribution, e-commerce, professional services | Healthcare, financial services, education, AI/tech |
| **Pricing** | More flexible for SME | Enterprise (higher cost) |
| **Customer list** | Smaller scale companies | 60% of Fortune 500 |

### Key takeaway for Eric (CPA)
- **Anthropic (fast-growing AI company, 2023-2026 hyper-scale) chose Workday over NetSuite**
- This signals: **AI-native companies prefer Workday's architecture for finance operations**
- NetSuite is solid for traditional mid-market; Workday is winning the AI/scale game

## 8. Bhusri's "Refounding" Strategy (2026)

Bhusri returned as CEO Feb 9, 2026. Key moves:

1. **Cut AI agents from 50 to 20** — focus on real ROI, not feature breadth
2. **Studied Steve Jobs' return to Apple** — "make Workday the biggest startup"
3. **Acquired Sana for $1.1B** — Swedish AI company, founder Joel Hellermark → Chief AI Officer
4. **Flattened organization** — "we don't need as many management layers because agents can do low-level management work"
5. **RAISE US** — early investor in retraining initiative (Gina Raimondo + Eric Holcomb)
6. **"Zero copy" AI training** — customer data stays in Workday, no IP leakage
