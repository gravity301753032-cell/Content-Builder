# LinkedIn Post — 你嘅人生係你所有決定嘅後果

**Your Life Is the Compound Interest of Every Choice You've Made**

There's a YouTube channel called School of Hard Knocks. Its founder, James Dumoulin, has a quote that stopped me cold:

*"When you're born, you look like your parents. But when you die, you look like your decisions."*

---

Think about Bezos's grandfather for a moment.

He made one decision on the side of a highway: not to scold his grandson, but to calmly say four words — *"It's harder to be kind than clever."*

One decision. Thirty years later, Bezos shared it in his Princeton commencement speech. Millions of people have now heard that story. One man's quiet choice in 1970s Texas is still compounding.

---

Wang Yangming's 知行合一 teaches us that knowing and doing are the same thing. Every choice is practice. Choose clever — you get better at clever. Choose kind — you get better at kind.

Most of us focus on the big decisions: which job to take, which city to live in, whether to start a company. But character isn't built in the big moments. It's built in the small ones. The conversation where you could be clever but choose to be kind. The feedback you could avoid giving but choose to deliver honestly. The truth you could hide behind a convenient excuse but choose to share.

---

*Talent is a gift. Character is a choice.*

And your life — the person you become, the legacy you leave — is the compound interest of every choice you've made.

Bezos's grandfather chose kind. What are you choosing today?
