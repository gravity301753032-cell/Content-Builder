## Post text (verbatim)

I spent 10 years in finance learning how to leverage systems.
Claude AI just made everything I knew feel slow.

3 shifts that hit different:
You're no longer the executor — you're the commander.
Tell AI what outcome you want. It uses the tools. You direct.

Knowledge acquisition cost is now near zero.
As someone who self-studied quant trading and Python on top of a full-time finance career — this is a bigger deal than most people realize.

Your output is no longer capped by your hours.
Claude Cowork + Claude Code run tasks while you sleep.
Solo founder? That's your team.
The workflow:
→ Chat → strategize
→ Cowork → execute (in parallel)
→ Code → ship the final product

Most people still use AI like Google.
Judgment and creativity are now the scarcest resources in business. Everything else can be delegated to AI.
What's one task you've fully handed off to AI? Curious what's working for people.
